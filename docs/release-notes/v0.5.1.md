# MT Verdant Nocturne 0.5.1

© Mayanktaker Computers & Web Development | https://mayanktaker.com

This release removes the grey haze that sat behind menus, notifications and the top bar.

## What's new

- **Menus sit on the wallpaper with a clean edge.** The soft shadow behind the calendar, quick settings and other popups is gone, so they no longer leave a smudged grey rectangle over light wallpapers.
- **Menus, the dash and notifications can be blurred by Blur My Shell.** Those surfaces are now slightly translucent, so Blur My Shell has something to work with and the blur actually shows behind them instead of a flat panel.
- **Checked quick settings buttons are flat.** They no longer cast a green glow around themselves.
- **The top bar no longer smudges what is under it.** Its shadow is gone in every top bar style: floating island, pill, bottom rounded and translucent.

## Bug fixes

- A large grey halo around the calendar, quick settings and notification popups is gone.
- The top bar no longer paints a dark band over the wallpaper below it.
- Dialog windows, the app switcher, on-screen displays and the overview no longer carry a shadow either.

## Notes

- Blur is optional. Without Blur My Shell the theme is translucent but flat, which is the intended look on its own.
- If you already had the theme installed, reinstall it once so the new surfaces are written.
- Icons and cursors continue to live in [mt-verdant-icons](https://github.com/Mayanktaker/mt-verdant-icons).
