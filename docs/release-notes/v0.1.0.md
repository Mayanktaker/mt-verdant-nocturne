# MT Verdant Nocturne v0.1.0

## What's new
- A fresh dark look for GNOME in quiet charcoal-teal with a leaf-sage highlight color, grown from a night-garden wallpaper.
- The top bar, quick settings, notifications, calendar, and overview get matching deep-teal surfaces with softer, easier-on-the-eyes contrast.
- An original "night garden" wallpaper is included — leaves in shadow, a faint moon glow, and a few pale blossoms. Applying it is always your choice.
- Classic GTK3 apps follow the new look too.

## Notes
- Designed and tested on GNOME 50 and 51.
- Some newer app windows (Files, Settings) keep their own styling by design; an optional extra can tint them to match.
- The top bar and menus change instantly; a few apps need to be reopened to pick up the theme.

## Bug fixes
- First release — nothing to fix yet.
